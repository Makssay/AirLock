# Business pitch for the final

Proposed model; pricing and demand await pilot validation.

## English, about 32 seconds at 130 words/minute

Our first customers are teams already automating treasury payouts. Airlock's contracts stay open source. We plan to charge for onboarding and operating the monitoring and guardian workflow: a proposed $500 setup fee and $99 per month for one treasury. Customers keep their keys and make the payout decisions. We are seeking three test-treasury pilot partners to measure operational effort and validate willingness to pay before launching the paid service.

## One-slide text

**Open contracts. Paid treasury operations.**

- Buyer: teams already running automated treasury payouts.
- Proposed Core price: $500 onboarding + $99/month per treasury.
- Target Team package: $249/month, up to three treasuries, after pilot validation.
- Customer control: keys and guardian decisions stay with the customer.
- Validation: five interviews → up to three scoped pilots → paid continuation.

Proposed pricing. No paid customers or revenue are claimed. Full assumptions and calculations: [BUSINESS-MODEL.md](BUSINESS-MODEL.md).

## Judge Q&A

**Why would someone pay if the contracts are open source?** We are testing demand for integration and an operated workflow: healthy observation, operator-funding checks, useful alerts, incident history and support. Teams can also run the code themselves.

**Why not just use a monitoring product?** Existing products already provide alerts. Our hypothesis is that some treasury teams want the payout policy and guardian process deployed and operated together on V3. We need pilot evidence to establish whether that convenience earns a subscription.

**Is $99 profitable?** In our planning model, a Core customer has $37.97/month of variable costs, including allocated support and maintenance time. Ten Core customers produce a $510.30 remainder after a $100 shared infrastructure allowance, before acquisition, core R&D and other excluded costs. These are scenarios, not measured margins. Two hours of monthly support instead of half an hour would almost eliminate the contribution.

**Can you sell it today?** We can demonstrate the prototype and offer a scoped test-environment design-partner pilot. A production managed V3 service needs deployment, operational validation and an agreed service scope before paid launch.
