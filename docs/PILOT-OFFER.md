# Airlock design-partner pilot

Copy-ready English material. Proposed offer, not an existing customer announcement. The team reviews and sends it; no outreach has been sent automatically.

## Short invitation

We are looking for up to three teams already automating treasury payouts to help validate Airlock on Portaldot V3.

Airlock adds a spending budget, a review window for larger requests and a separate guardian who can pause or cancel a payout. The pilot covers one test treasury, the supported reference source flow, Telegram alerts and a repeatable operator exercise.

The proposed pilot lasts 14 days, with up to six hours of setup and support from our team at no service charge. We start with the public playground and a review of your workflow. A live V3 test starts once network access, test funds and operator budgets are confirmed. Your team keeps its keys and guardian decisions.

After a successful pilot, our proposed Core service is $99 per treasury per month, with standard onboarding normally priced at $500. Pilot onboarding is waived within the agreed scope. Custom source adapters and additional work are scoped separately. These prices are being validated; there is no automatic renewal or payment collection.

Would you be open to a 20-minute workflow interview and a test-treasury pilot?

## Scope and acceptance

| Stage | Work | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Days 1–2 | Identify the payout owner, source flow, approval rules and guardian | Written scope and short baseline interview |
| Days 3–5 | Configure the agreed test environment and check operator funding | Policy, role separation, environment labels and funding preflight |
| Days 6–10 | Exercise normal, delayed and stopped requests; inspect notifications and interruption recovery | Receipts or explicit simulation records; notification and manual-support timings |
| Days 11–14 | Review usability, costs and willingness to continue | Customer feedback, time ledger and a decision on paid continuation |

The pilot runs in the playground or a funded testnet. Real assets/mainnet are outside its initial scope. If V3 funding is unavailable, record that gate and complete simulation/workflow validation without presenting it as live V3 evidence. Total Airlock effort is capped at six hours; scope changes require a new agreement.

Automated observation and delivery are evaluated separately from customer guardian response. Customer keys stay with the customer. Network fees and storage deposits are funded separately; they are not subscription revenue. No audit, insurance or round-the-clock human response service is included in this pilot.

## Interview questions

1. Describe the last time you ran an automated payout. Who owned the process and which tools were involved?
2. How do you currently limit a mistaken or compromised relay instruction?
3. Which payouts can wait, and who can reliably respond during that interval?
4. What would your current process do if an RPC or relay stopped halfway through a payout?
5. How much engineering/operations time does this process take in a normal month? Ask for an example rather than supply a number.
6. What other tools or internal scripts would Airlock need to work with?
7. Who approves a recurring operational-tools purchase? What would they need to see?
8. After demonstrating the workflow, ask which parts they would pay to have operated and whether $99/month plus standard onboarding fits their process. Record objections and the next concrete decision.

## Pilot scorecard

Record the environment, number of requests, outcome, time to observe, time accepted by the notification provider, human acknowledgement, transaction finality, false/duplicate alerts, RPC interruptions, setup time and support time. Use a distinct test ID for each exercise. Capture price feedback verbatim with permission; do not count general enthusiasm as a purchase commitment.

Target signal: five workflow interviews, up to three scoped pilots, two concrete pricing discussions and one agreed paid continuation after technical prerequisites are met. Actual received payment is the revenue milestone. No such results are claimed yet.
