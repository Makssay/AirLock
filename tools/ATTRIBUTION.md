# Third-party validator

`portawasm.py` is copied unmodified from https://github.com/jonathan-moore58/portaldot-contract-zero/blob/main/tools/portawasm.py on 16 October 2026, under the MIT license in PORTAWASM-LICENSE.

It checks a subset of the published legacy runtime's structural and instruction constraints. Its “ACCEPTED” output is an offline check, not a guarantee of deployment, runtime behavior or security. Airlock's contract code is original and is executed separately by the WASM host tests.
